Eight Reasons most remodeling websites lose homeowners to Houzz and Angi

Home remodelers compete against aggregators (Angi, Houzz, Thumbtack, HomeAdvisor) and chains. Most remodeling sites we audit are leaking qualified design-consultation requests in ways the owner hasn't noticed yet.

No Project Gallery Organized by Room Type

Kitchen, bath, basement, addition, outdoor homeowners shop by room. Sites with one mixed gallery cannot rank for kitchen remodel queries separately from bath remodel.

No project budget transparency

Average kitchen remodel ranges from $25K to $150K depending on scope. Sites that publish ranges (starter, mid-range, high-end) qualify serious prospects and filter tire-kickers.

License and insurance info buried in the footer

State contractor license, liability insurance, workers comp, bonding — trust signals visible where homeowners actually look for $40K+ projects.

No financing partnership visibles

GreenSky, Hearth, Service Finance, Synchrony Home — without financing partnerships visible, leveraged-buy homeowners book the competitor showing as-low-as $X/month messaging.

No process explanation

First-time remodelers do not know what to expect. Discovery call → on-site visit → design → permits → demolition → install → punch-list answers the silent objection that kills consultation requests.

Generic 5-tips-for-your-kitchen blog content

Real remodeling SEO covers specific project breakdowns (what a $60K kitchen actually includes, IKEA vs custom cabinets, quartz vs granite ROI) with depth.

No designer or in-house design positioning

Design-build remodelers (in-house design + install) win the higher-margin prospect when they surface it clearly. Subcontracted-design remodelers should not pretend otherwise.

Quote form takes 12 fields and 4 minutes

Long forms kill conversion. Smart forms collect room type, budget range, timeline, ZIP, and project description in 5 fields, route into BuilderTrend or CoConstruct.

Website design built around your specific remodelers niche

A kitchen-and-bath specialist and a whole-home renovation generalist sell entirely different services to entirely different buyers. We design for the niche.

Kitchen remodeling specialist

Budget tier transparency, cabinet line partnerships (KraftMaid, Wood-Mode, Brookhaven, IKEA-custom), countertop comparison, project case studies by style.

Bathroom remodeling specialist

Half-bath vs full vs primary-suite pricing tiers, shower vs tub configurations, tile and fixture brand partnerships, before-and-after gallery by bath type.

Whole-home remodeler

Multi-phase project messaging, $200K+ budget transparency, design-build positioning, project case studies with full-home transformations, financing partnerships.

Basement finishing specialist

Egress requirements, waterproofing, ceiling height considerations, layout templates (rec room, guest suite, theater, bar), code-aware permitting.

Addition specialist

Bump-out vs full addition pricing, foundation considerations, roof tie-in expertise, HOA and zoning navigation, structural engineering relationships.

ADU and accessory dwelling specialist

State-specific ADU regulations (CA, OR, WA, MA), JADU vs ADU vs detached, financing approaches, rental income ROI content, prefab vs custom positioning.

Outdoor living specialist

Outdoor kitchens, covered patios, pool surrounds, fire features, three-season rooms — extending livable square footage, seasonal install scheduling.

Aging-in-place specialist

CAPS-certified remodeler credentials, universal design positioning, accessibility modifications (grab bars, zero-threshold showers, wider doorways), aging-in-place tax incentives.
